# Primary royalty extraction — evidence first (subscription CLI, no model API)

You are extracting economic facts from ONE mapped public licensing document for a professional valuation research workbook. You are not a source of evidence. Use ONLY the supplied numbered passage packet and cited SEC document. Do not use internet guesses. Do not invent dates, rates or identities.

## Inputs
- `packet.json` contains exact `document_id`, `source_text_sha256`, candidate IDs, passage text, section inventory and source URL.
- `m2_extraction.schema.json` describes the exact output object. Fill required keys, using null for unsupported optional values.
- If there are multiple packet chunks, **read every assigned chunk** before claiming to have read those passage IDs. Do not claim FULL_DOCUMENT for selected packets. If capacity stops you reading all candidates, output an exception instead of a completed result.

## Mandatory extraction method
1. Read every supplied candidate passage and relevant surrounding text. Inspect payment, royalty, sublicense, fees, definitions, schedules, exceptions, reductions, adjustments, minimums and amendments. Do not stop after the first rate.
2. Extract **one royalty term per distinct royalty** (same payer, royalty base and family: sales royalty, sublicense receipts, per-unit, usage, revenue share). Do not merge sublicense receipts into sales royalty rates, and keep different bases separate (for example Net Sales, Government Sales and Licensed Software Products are different royalties). Within one royalty:
   - rate steps by threshold, period or volume go in that term's `tiers` (one tier per step, with the threshold in `threshold_basis` and its evidence);
   - a rate that depends on a condition (for example which rate applies when annual Government Sales are below or above a threshold) is the same royalty: record it once, with each variant as a tier (or a condition), not as another term;
   - anti-stacking, third-party royalty offsets, royalty reductions, credits, floors ("not below X%") and other adjustments of that royalty go in that term's `adjustments`, **never as a separate term**; put the figures in the description and cite the evidence;
   - record each royalty only **once**. If a chunk shows a royalty without its rate (the schedule is in another chunk), record it once with `rate_unit=UNKNOWN` and add an open issue (`CROSS_CHUNK_ECONOMICS`) naming it; Python reconciles an unrated term with the same family and base quoted in another chunk and lists every such merge in the QC sheet. Do not record the same royalty again for the sake of completeness.
3. Preserve any percentages associated with interest, penalties, ownership, audit tolerances etc. as an explicitly classified unrelated percentage, not as royalty terms.
4. Preserve clauses with rate redactions by writing `disclosure_status=REDACTED`, `rate_value=null`, and `rate_unit=REDACTED` with an exact source quotation.
5. Every material field and every condition must have source evidence. An `evidence` quote must be an exact substring of the corresponding passage text. Make it the shortest clause-level span that shows the figure and its basis (prefer at least a few words, not a bare "4%"); a quote under 8 characters is accepted but flagged with a warning. Use evidence IDs to cite each rate, base, threshold and trigger independently; **never claim that a quote being present proves the economics are correct**.
6. Assign every `candidate_passage_id` exactly one disposition record. A passage can have multiple `categories`; explain if it is unrelated or duplicate. Every extracted payment/royalty item must link to a real term/payment ID. Do not silently discard a candidate.
7. Add explicit unresolved issues for interpretation uncertainty, unavailable definitions, cross-references, potential missing schedules, amendment history and anything that requires external review.
8. Set `reader.read_scope=SELECTED_PASSAGES` unless you genuinely received and read every passage in a full packet set. Set `reviewed_passage_ids` to those actually read, not a synthetic list.
9. Amendments are not assumed checked; usually set `amendment_status=NOT_CHECKED` or `REFERENCED_UNRESOLVED`. A historical SEC filing does not establish current operative status.
10. Complete licensing-rights metadata from source quotations, using the optional agreement fields in the schema: `licensed_ip_nature` (TRADEMARK_BRAND, PATENT, COPYRIGHT, SOFTWARE, KNOW_HOW, TECHNOLOGY, TRADE_SECRET, FRANCHISE, MIXED, OTHER or UNDETERMINED; TECHNOLOGY means unpatented technology or technical information); `licensed_ip_nature_basis`; `licensed_ip_components`; `licensed_ip_nature_details`; `sublicensing_rights`; `related_party`; and `other_material_terms` (category, text and evidence IDs; see the rule below on what to record). Cite `agreement.field_evidence` for any populated positive licensing fields.
    - **IA nature:** pick the best-supported category and state the basis in `licensed_ip_nature_basis`: `STATED` when the text names the type of IP; `INFERRED` when it does not but context points to one (for example a term running to patent expiration, or the licensed asset being a drug candidate, device or software). For INFERRED, cite that evidence under `field_evidence.licensed_ip_nature` and explain it in `licensed_ip_nature_details`. Use UNDETERMINED (basis null) only when there is no clue at all. Do not infer trademark status from a name alone.
    - **IA components:** when more than one type of IP is licensed, set `licensed_ip_nature` to MIXED **and** list every licensed type in `licensed_ip_components` (values from TRADEMARK_BRAND, PATENT, COPYRIGHT, SOFTWARE, KNOW_HOW, TECHNOLOGY, TRADE_SECRET, FRANCHISE; never MIXED/OTHER/UNDETERMINED). Each listed component must be supported by cited evidence under `field_evidence.licensed_ip_components` (or `licensed_ip_nature`); an inferred component is allowed under the same STATED/INFERRED basis rule, explained in `licensed_ip_nature_details`. For a single type the list may hold that one type (or be null).
    - **Related party:** `YES` only when the text says the parties are related or affiliated or calls the agreement a related-party transaction (cite the evidence); `NO_STATED_ARMS_LENGTH` when it states the parties are unrelated or the deal is at arm's length (cite it); `NOT_STATED` when the text is silent.
    - **Other material terms:** record terms that matter to a royalty valuation, and supplementary terms that may affect it. Valuation-relevant examples: licensed asset, scope or field; exclusivity; royalty base or computation; rate step-downs, reductions, offsets or stacking; minimum payments and diligence obligations; term, expiry and early-termination rights (for example a right to terminate if there are no sales within five years); costs borne by the licensee (for example patent prosecution and maintenance); sublicensing economics; most-favoured terms; change-of-control effects on economics. Supplementary examples: payment mechanics, currency and FX; reporting; audit; insolvency or bankruptcy; assignment or transfer restrictions; governing law of payments; warranties and indemnities with caps. Do **not** record pure admin boilerplate: use of the licensor's name or marks in publicity, notices, confidentiality boilerplate, counterparts, entire agreement, headings and similar. Economic terms still go in the royalty/payment fields as before. Do **not** list `other_material_terms` under `agreement.field_evidence`: each term carries its own `evidence_ids`.
    - Do not assume non-exclusivity or sublicensing prohibition from silence, and use null where unsupported. Document economic terms in the existing royalty/payment fields rather than only in `other_material_terms`.
11. Return **only one valid JSON object** matching `m2_extraction.schema.json`, without code fences or commentary.

## Warning
An extraction with accurate individual rates can still be **materially incomplete**. The validator checks quotation presence and candidate bookkeeping, not genuine full-document recall. An independent full-read pass and human-reviewed benchmark are required before accuracy claims.
