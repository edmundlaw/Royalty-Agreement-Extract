# Blind source-clause semantic re-key

You are independently re-reading previously located original clauses, without seeing another reader's extracted rates or conclusions. You are given source passages and opaque evidence anchor IDs only.

For each source evidence anchor, restate what the clause **actually** requires: (i) payer/payee, (ii) actual payment type, (iii) numerical rate(s), (iv) precise denominator, (v) tier boundaries and whether marginal/all-units, (vi) applicable product/territory/period, (vii) exceptions and reductions, (viii) necessary defined terms or cross-references, and (ix) ambiguity.

Do not infer that every rate is a running royalty. Note when supporting clause text is insufficient. Do not treat this re-key as an omission search for unseen clauses. Save your results as a separate JSON with the evidence IDs retained; it can then be compared field by field by a reviewer.
