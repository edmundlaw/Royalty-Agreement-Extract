# v0.13.12 changes

Fixes from an external test round of v0.13.11 (Laser Lock-Copag and Voit-Sport Supply read live, plus the v0.13.10 patent licence). Each claim was reproduced on v0.13.11 code before it was fixed: items 1-4 by an offline finalize of a small hand-made reading (`tests/fixtures/v01312`, verbatim snippets of the public agreements) and by inspecting the supplied workbooks; item 5 from the supplied workbook (row height 33, vertical centre). No AI reader was run.

- **1. Sell-off restatement.** Reproduced: Voit `C1-R1` and `C4-R1` (24-month Sell-Off Period, s.13(h)) both exported Usable = YES with no link. Now a royalty term whose OWN wording (royalty period, product scope, territory, base, its rate quotation or its conditions; not its adjustments) says sell-off / "sell off", or a disposal of inventory after termination or expiration ("six months after expiration, for the disposal of inventory on hand"), is period-limited. Sheet 01 has two new columns: `Period-limited` (`YES (sell-off)`) and `Linked to row` (the base row's observation ID; the base row says `has period-limited restatement: <id>`). `Usable for royalty benchmark` = NO with first reason `period-limited (sell-off) restatement of <id> - use base row`. The rows are never merged and no figure is changed. The link is made only when exactly one plausible base term exists (same document and family, not itself period-limited; several candidates are narrowed by royalty base and product scope). Otherwise the row is not linked, still NO (`period-limited (...) royalty; base row not identified`), and a MEDIUM / OPEN issue of kind `PERIOD_LIMITED_LINK_UNRESOLVED` is added to sheet 05. Follow-up (review of 34b2c4f): the signal is split. STRONG = sell-off wording in the term's own period, scope, territory, base, base definition or rate quotation; WEAK = wording only in its conditions (a base royalty clause often mentions the sell-off in passing). A term is period-limited only with a STRONG signal, or a WEAK one when a term with no sell-off wording at all exists to link to. A flagged term with no base candidate is NOT a restatement: `Period-limited` stays blank, the rule does not set Usable = NO, and a LOW / OPEN issue `PERIOD_LIMITED_WORDING_NO_BASE` ("kept as a base row - check at point of use") is added. The MEDIUM `PERIOD_LIMITED_LINK_UNRESOLVED` issue now applies only when several candidate bases stay tied. Wording that does NOT trigger: "royalties continue after expiry of the patent", "during the Term", "Net Sales after expiration of the Initial Term", "sales of inventory during the Term" (negative tests).
- **2. Wrapped quotes.** Reproduced: Voit rate quote "four percent (4%) of all Net Sales during that Contract Year (...) minus" (one wrapped line), and the s.11(b) late-charge payment (no amount) showed the line without the "1%". Two changes, display only. (a) `_best_evidence`: a payment without an amount now prefers the evidence quote that contains a percent or money figure written in the record's own Basis / Condition (the "1%" of the late charge); then RATE-role evidence, then the first. (b) New `display_quote()`: the chosen quote is completed to its enclosing sentence using the text of the cited passage and the passages next to it (a wrapped clause continues in the next line). The shown text is the source text itself (whitespace normalised) and at most 500 characters (raised from 300 after review: the Laser per-unit rate lost its "in excess of 200,000 units" lead); a longer sentence is cut at clause boundaries (`, ; :`) with an ellipsis, trimming the side further from the quote first so the quote stays roughly centred. It never reaches across a page-number or ALL-CAPS heading line, does not end a sentence at "Inc.", "U.S.", "No." or a single capital, and leaves the quote alone when it cannot be found inside the cited passage. Stored evidence (sheet 04, `rows.json`) and the source check are unchanged. Applies to sheet 01 `Rate quotation` and sheet 03 `First source quotation`.
- **3. Per-unit rate in the percent column.** Reproduced: Laser `$0.05` was written to `Royalty rate (%)` (value 0.05). Now `Royalty rate (%)` (sheet 01) and `Rate numeric` (sheet 02) hold only PERCENT / PCT units. New column `Per-unit / currency rate` (sheet 01, after the percent column; sheet 02, after `Rate unit`) shows text such as `0.05 USD per unit`; the currency is read from the figure as written (`$0.05`, `USD 2.50`, a GBP sign) in the reported value, else in the rate quotation, a bare `$` is read as USD, and when no currency is written next to the figure the text says `(currency not stated)`. A numeric rate with another unit (FORMULA, UNKNOWN) shows as `<n> (rate unit X)` in that column. `Rate_Numeric` in `rows.json` / CSV is unchanged. Flags unchanged (`Usable` = NO, `rate is not a percentage`). `scripts/verify_research_export_parity.py` now expects an empty percent cell for non-percent units, checks the per-unit text starts with the same number, and ignores the Python-raised `PERIOD_LIMITED_LINK_UNRESOLVED` issues when counting sheet 05.
- **4. Advance / prepayment, Upfront fee.** Checked the sources first. Voit s.4(a) says "the Minimum Royalty to be paid in advance on or before January 30th of each Contract Year": the tester is right and the reader's payment record says `prepaid by January 30 each Contract Year`. Laser s.4.03 says the Minimum Annual Royalty "shall be paid ... on the Effective Date, and each anniversary date thereof": that is a due date, there is no advance wording, so the tester's claim is an inference and is NOT applied. Rule: a minimum-royalty payment record (or a GUARANTEE explicitly labelled a minimum royalty) whose own Basis, Condition or evidence quotation has explicit wording ("in advance", "advance payment / royalty", "prepaid", "payable / paid upon signing / execution") fills `Advance / prepayment` with the amount, the payment ID and the wording, e.g. `USD 130,000 [C1-P1] (minimum royalty record says: "prepaid by January 30 each Contract Year")`. A clause about notice, mail or "in writing" is ignored ("90 days notice in advance"). A genuine ADVANCE record is kept and listed first. UPFRONT is never derived: neither agreement has a signing fee. When payment records exist but none is an advance / upfront fee the cell now reads `NOT_ASSESSED (checked N payment records: none is an advance or prepayment; absence is not proven by one reading)` (still starts with NOT_ASSESSED; with no payment records at all it stays plain `NOT_ASSESSED`). One existing test assertion was relaxed from equality to starts-with for this.
- **5. Row height.** Reproduced: the supplied workbooks have height 33 and vertically centred cells (Voit `Sublicensing rights` is 276 characters in a width-38 column). Data rows on sheets 00-06 now get a height estimated from the text length and the column width (minimum 33 on sheets 01 / 05 and 30 elsewhere, maximum 150 points), wrap text stays on, and cells are aligned to the top so a cell that still exceeds 150 points shows its beginning. ID lists, SHA and URL columns are left out of the estimate (they would make every row 150 points). The house style file has no row-height rule; fonts, gridlines and colours are unchanged.
- **6. `m3_chunks.py` cross-chunk header conflicts.** Not reproduced as "silent": when two reading tasks give different values for an agreement field the first value is kept AND an open issue `AGREEMENT_HEADER_DIFFERS_ACROSS_AI_TASKS` (MEDIUM / OPEN) with both values is added since v0.8.1 (it already appears on sheet 05; ColdQuanta, Kala and NeurogesX saved readings show it). What was missing: the issue named the later task only. It now also names the task the kept value came from (`Kept (task 1, the first to give a value): ... Task 2 gave: ...`). New tests prove the kept value, the issue, equal values raising nothing, and the issue reaching sheet 05. Not changed: the merged `field_evidence` of a conflicting later task is still added to the kept field (it may cite evidence for the dropped wording); this is recorded here, not changed.
- **7. Minimum without its qualifier.** Reproduced from the v0.13.10 workbook: the payment record's Basis says `Annual minimum royalty per licensed Product`, the main row shows `USD 25,000 [1-PAY02]`. The main-row `Minimum guarantee` now carries a `per ...` / `for each|every ...` phrase taken from the payment's own Basis (cut at the first filler word): `USD 25,000 per licensed Product [1-PAY02]`. The Condition is not used (Voit's "may be increased once per completed five-year period" is about increases, not the minimum), and nothing is inferred when the Basis has no such phrase (Voit and Laser minimums unchanged).
- Not changed: reader interpretation; the tier path (not exercised by the tester). The patent licence was not re-read; item 7 is tested on payment records shaped like the v0.13.10 output, not on a fresh reading. `scripts/verify_release.py` and `RELEASE_SHA256.json` were not regenerated for 0.13.12.

# v0.13.11 changes

Fixes from an external test round of v0.13.10 (two public SEC agreements read end to end, plus synthetic probes). Each claim was reproduced on v0.13.10 before it was fixed.

- **A1 short quotes.** The hard schema rule `evidence.quote minLength 8` (both schema copies) is now `minLength 1`, and the source check no longer rejects a quote under 8 characters unless it is the whole passage. A short quote is still checked as an exact part of the cited passage; it gets the per-record warning `M13_QUOTE_VERY_SHORT`. A document is never SOURCE_CHECK_FAILED for short quotes alone. FIRST_READER asks for the shortest clause-level span showing the figure and its basis.
- **A2 cross-chunk duplicates.** `_fold_unrated` ranks the candidates (exact normalised product scope, then exact territory) and folds when exactly one is best. "All other X" / "remaining" scopes are not compatible with a specific named scope. If candidates stay tied nothing is attached: the unrated row stays, sheet 01 says `unresolved cross-chunk duplicate - see sheet 05` (benchmark NO) and a HIGH / OPEN open issue of kind `UNRESOLVED_DUPLICATE` is added.
- **A3 tiered label.** A TIERED / HYBRID / FORMULA term with disclosed tiers shows `TIERED 4%-7% - see sheet 02` (min-max of the tier rates) in `Royalty rate as reported`; the numeric rate cell stays blank (no tiers collapsed, sorting unchanged). Benchmark note: `tiered rate - assess at point of use` (still NO) instead of `rate not disclosed or unclear`. Redacted / ambiguous terms keep the old note.
- **A4 quotation choice.** Payment `First source quotation` and the sheet-01 `Rate quotation` prefer an evidence quote containing the reported amount (digits compared after normalising `$20,000`, `20000`, `9%`), then RATE-role evidence, then the first.
- **B1** two or more default (no threshold) terms no longer block merging of the threshold variants of the same group (the defaults stay separate); one default plus variants still merges. **B2** a phase split whose conditions START with until / prior to / before / after / thereafter / beginning / commencing / following now merges as tiers ("payable within 30 days after ..." does not). **B3** an OTHER / FORMULA reduction without a term ID is attached when the reading has exactly one royalty term. **B4** the minimum-guarantee label may open the Basis or the Condition field. **B5** VARIANTS never merge terms with different rate units (previously the merged term got unit UNKNOWN).
- **B6 tier bounds.** Merged tiers read `lower` / `upper` (less than, fewer than, below, under, up to, not more than, more than, greater than, exceed(s), in excess of, above, over, at least, "X or more/less", between X and Y; $ / USD / thousand / million), `calculation` MARGINAL / ALL_UNITS when the text says so (that portion, incremental, retroactive ...) and `measurement_period` (annual, calendar year ...). A bound is written only if the same number appears in the cited quotation (so the source check cannot newly fail). Tiers with a threshold but no readable bound (redacted share, non-numeric) are named in the CONSOLIDATED note and a LOW / OPEN `TIER_BOUNDS_NOT_PARSED` issue. `K` abbreviations ("$250K") are not read.
- **C1** `_number()` strips a trailing `%` / `percent` / `per cent` for rate fields ("5%", "5.5 %" now export 5; amounts and thresholds are unchanged).
- Not changed: A5 (reader interpretation). `scripts/verify_release.py` and `RELEASE_SHA256.json` were not regenerated for 0.13.11.

# v0.13.10 changes

One royalty is now one row. Python (zero AI tokens) consolidates the first reading after the chunk merge and before the source check / export (`m13_consolidate.py`, called from `finalize`). When a rule is not confident the terms stay separate.

- **Chunk duplicates folded.** A term with no rate quotation and no numeric rate (rate UNKNOWN, e.g. "Net Sales of Licensed Products", seen in chunk 1 before the schedule) is folded into the ONE quoted term of the same family and same royalty base (words compared after dropping filler such as licensed / products / patent rights and the text in brackets; sublicense, revenue and sales words are normalised; product scope and territory must not conflict). Evidence, conditions and adjustments are unioned; the quoted term's ID stays. More than one possible target means no fold.
- **Threshold variants become tiers.** Terms with the same family, base, product scope (text before the first `;`), territory and period that differ ONLY by a threshold condition (less than / more than / or more / exceed ...) become one TIERED term with one tier per variant (rate, threshold text in `threshold_basis`, evidence kept). At most one variant may have no threshold (the default). Different bases or scopes (Net Sales vs Government Sales vs Software Products) are never merged.
- **Reductions become adjustments.** A term whose own text is a reduction / offset / anti-stacking / floor (wording in its base or scope, or a formula / OTHER term without a numeric rate that carries that wording) becomes an adjustment on the royalty it reduces, keeping the figures, conditions and all evidence. The target is the term whose adjustments or conditions name it by term ID, or else the only royalty term of the same family.
- **Nothing disappears silently.** Every merge is a RESOLVED / LOW open issue of kind `CONSOLIDATED` (sheet 05), e.g. `CONSOLIDATED: 1-t1 folded into c2-t1 (same family and base, no rate quoted)`. The output folder also holds `consolidation.json` and `first_reader_extraction_before_consolidation.json` (only when something was consolidated). Removed term IDs are replaced by the kept ID in the passage dispositions.
- **Source check and two readers.** The source check runs on the consolidated reading (all evidence IDs are preserved). The two-reader comparison uses the RAW readings of both readers (before consolidation), so it is symmetrical and unchanged.
- FIRST_READER: one term per distinct royalty; rate steps and condition variants in `tiers`; anti-stacking, offsets, floors and reductions in `adjustments`, never a separate term; a royalty seen without its rate is recorded once with a cross-chunk open issue.
- Real data: ColdQuanta Government Sales licence 5 -> 3 royalty rows, ColdQuanta chunked licence 5 -> 3, VXM01 and N2OFF unchanged (3 and 1). Kala (anti-stacking) 10 -> 8 and KemPharm 3 -> 1 in saved readings.
- `scripts/verify_release.py` and `RELEASE_SHA256.json` were not regenerated for 0.13.10.

# v0.13.9 changes

- **Rates are real percentages in Excel.** Every rate cell (sheet 01 `Royalty rate (%)`, sheet 02 `Rate numeric`) now holds the fraction (5 percent-points -> 0.05) with number format `0.00%` (shows 5.00%; 12.5 shows 12.50%), so formulas get 0.05 and viewers no longer show 500%. Only rows with unit PERCENT/PCT are converted; per-unit amounts, FORMULA and UNKNOWN stay as they are. `Rate_Numeric` in rows.json / CSV and the benchmark rule (50 or more) stay in percent-points. The legacy 16-sheet technical workbook writes no formatted rate cells and is unchanged. `scripts/verify_research_export_parity.py` compares the cell with `Rate_Numeric / 100` for percent units (also for tiers).
- **Mixed IA lists every component.** New optional `agreement.licensed_ip_components` (array of TRADEMARK_BRAND, PATENT, COPYRIGHT, SOFTWARE, KNOW_HOW, TECHNOLOGY, TRADE_SECRET, FRANCHISE) and new nature value `TECHNOLOGY` (unpatented technology / technical information), in both schema copies. FIRST_READER rule 10: more than one IP type -> nature MIXED and every component listed with evidence (inferred allowed under the STATED/INFERRED rule). Chunk merge takes the union of the lists; the source check requires cited evidence (under `licensed_ip_components`, or under `licensed_ip_nature`; neighbouring fields when INFERRED), like the nature. `Nature of IA` shows e.g. `MIXED: patent, know-how, trademark/brand (inferred)`. Old readings with MIXED but no list, or UNDETERMINED, list ALL matched types as `MIXED: patent, know-how/trade secret, ... (keyword hint)` (patent, know-how/trade secret, technology/technical information, software/source code, copyright, trademark/brand/trade name/logo, franchise); a reader's own non-UNDETERMINED value is never overridden. Single types are unchanged.
- **Other terms: valuation-focused.** Python assigns each other material term a `Valuation relevance` tier (rules documented in `m13_research_export.py`, `classify_other_term`): KEY (licensed asset/scope/field, exclusivity, royalty base, step-downs/offsets/stacking, minimums/diligence, term and termination rights such as no sales within 5 years, licensee-borne costs such as patent prosecution/maintenance, sublicensing economics, most-favoured terms, change of control with economic effect), SUPPLEMENTARY (payment mechanics/FX, reporting, audit, insolvency, assignment/transfer, governing law, warranties/indemnities), ADMIN (publicity / use of the licensor's name, notices, confidentiality, counterparts, entire agreement, headings and similar). Rules run in order: KEY keywords, strong ADMIN keywords, weak ADMIN keywords (category OTHER only), then the reader's category (SCOPE/EXCLUSIVITY/SUBLICENSING/TERMINATION/RENEWAL -> KEY, else SUPPLEMENTARY). Unsure -> SUPPLEMENTARY; nothing is dropped.
- Sheet 01 `Other material terms` lists only KEY terms, short (at most 120 characters), e.g. `TERM/TERMINATION, COSTS, DILIGENCE (4 key terms) - see sheet 06`, or `no key terms (n other terms) - see sheet 06`. Sheet 06 gets a `Valuation relevance` column after Category, rows sorted KEY, SUPPLEMENTARY, ADMIN (filters kept), ADMIN rows in grey font.
- FIRST_READER: `other_material_terms` should record valuation-relevant and supplementary terms and not pure admin boilerplate (saves output tokens); economic terms still go in the royalty/payment fields.
- FIRST_READER also tells the reader not to list `other_material_terms` under `agreement.field_evidence` (each term has its own `evidence_ids`); the merge additionally accepts an exact redundant entry (live run 2026-01-05..16).
- Fix: `royalty.sh` named a workbook `_v0.8.1` in a live run (package was 0.13.8). It imported the package with `python -I`, which ignores PYTHONPATH and found an old editable install of another checkout.
